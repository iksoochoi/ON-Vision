Shoe segmentation model (best.pt): YOLO11n-seg, one class "shoe", all five cameras in one model. Version 3 (2026-11-09).
Made on the PC in train/shoe_segmentation (not in git): 73 epochs (stopped early, best at epoch 52), imgsz 960, RTX 4060, 64 minutes.

Why: the site got a dark room (2026-11-02). Version 2 (trained before it) found no shoe in 117 of 215 dark-room pictures, mostly green shoes.

Train: 2577 pictures.
       1285 of 2026-11-02, dark room (labels data/labels_seg1102_2026-11-08, mask with a 15 px margin; about 6:4 green:grey shoes):
            top 256, back 253, left 262, front 260, right 254.
       1292 of 2026-10-24, before the dark room (the training pictures of version 2).
Val:   215 pictures of 92 other 2026-11-02 shoe passes (all cameras of a pass on the same side, chosen per shoe colour):
            top 44, back 47, left 38, front 40, right 46.
       And the version 2 val (227 pictures of 2026-10-24), not trained on, to check the old room is still known.

Result like the service (imgsz 960, conf 0.5, the most certain shoe), mean IoU with the label / pictures without a shoe:
       2026-11-02 dark room: 0.980 / 0 (worst 0.940; top 0.969, back 0.973, front 0.984, right 0.987, left 0.988;
                             green 0.981, grey 0.978). Version 2: 0.390 / 117.
       2026-10-24 old room:  0.980 / 0 (worst 0.911). Version 2: 0.978 / 0.
       A model on the 2026-11-02 pictures only did as well in the dark room but forgot the old room (0.869, 12 without a shoe).
Speed: same model size as version 2 (about 20 ms per picture on an RTX 4060).

The 2026-11-02 labels were made with SAM2.1 and not all checked by a person; the val uses the same kind of labels.
Mask = the shoe (upper + sole) without the last (teal), the metal holder and the belt.

Versions 1 (2026-10-27) and 2 (2026-10-29, 2026-10-24 pictures only) are in the git history of this file.

yolo11n-seg.pt in train/shoe_segmentation is the general starting model of Ultralytics (80 COCO classes), NOT this model: it is not used.
